# OpenShot (GPL-3)

Packages built from material of [OpenShot](https://github.com/OpenShot/openshot-qt), licensed under the GNU GPL version 3 or later ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). Each package carries its own LICENSE and a README naming the source files and commit. Nothing here is shared with the MIT packages.

The 405 luma images of `src/transitions/extra` are split by name over ten luma wipe packages, so that opening one loads only its own images (29 to 53 of them, at most some 17 MiB on the GPU):

- [luma-wipe-twirl](luma-wipe-twirl/README.md): 53 OpenShot luma images: twirls, whirlpools and spirals
- [luma-wipe-luminous](luma-wipe-luminous/README.md): 44 OpenShot luma images: glows, suns and stars
- [luma-wipe-ray](luma-wipe-ray/README.md): 29 OpenShot luma images: rays and blurred bars
- [luma-wipe-distortion](luma-wipe-distortion/README.md): 42 OpenShot luma images: distortions and deformations
- [luma-wipe-ripple](luma-wipe-ripple/README.md): 31 OpenShot luma images: ripples, waves, fish eyes and stretches
- [luma-wipe-bars](luma-wipe-bars/README.md): 35 OpenShot luma images: bars, blinds and diagonal wipes
- [luma-wipe-shapes](luma-wipe-shapes/README.md): 49 OpenShot luma images: crosses, triangles, arrows and other shapes
- [luma-wipe-mosaic](luma-wipe-mosaic/README.md): 42 OpenShot luma images: boards, boxes and mosaics
- [luma-wipe-organic](luma-wipe-organic/README.md): 45 OpenShot luma images: fog, clouds, flowers, fractals and stains
- [luma-wipe-abstract](luma-wipe-abstract/README.md): 35 OpenShot luma images: middle, future, postime and inspiration patterns
